Chassis & Drivetrain Engineering

The M xDrive all-wheel-drive system in the BMW M4 Competition employs an electronically controlled multi-plate clutch in the transfer case to ensure fully variable, seamless distribution of torque between the front and rear axles. In default 4WD mode, the system maintains optimum traction without sacrificing rear-bias agility.

Switching to 4WD Sport transmits an even greater proportion of torque to the rear wheels during racetrack maneuvers, permitting controlled, safe power slides. For purists seeking absolute mechanical connection, selecting 2WD completely disengages the front drive axle and deactivates stability control, unleashing pure rear-wheel-drive drifts.
