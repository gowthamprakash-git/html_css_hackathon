The M Performance Lineup: Uncompromising Power

For over fifty years, the letter M has stood as the definitive badge of high-performance automotive achievement. Developed under extreme motorsport conditions at the Nürburgring Nordschleife, BMW M vehicles translate championship technology into exhilarating street-legal machinery.

Every BMW M automobile is built with high-revving M TwinPower Turbo inline-six engines, razor-sharp 50:50 axle weight balance, adaptive M dampers, and functional aerodynamic cooling channels that ensure consistent lap times in summer heat.

Core Pillars of BMW M Engineering

  • M TwinPower Turbo Inline-6: High-revving 3.0-liter engines with forged lightweight crankshafts and twin mono-scroll turbochargers.
  • Adaptive M Suspension: Electronically controlled dampers that continuously adapt damping characteristics in milliseconds.
  • Active M Differential: Electro-mechanical multi-plate clutch locking system ensuring optimum traction under extreme cornering.
  • M Carbon Ceramic Brakes: Extreme thermal tolerance, fade-free braking power, and significant unsprung weight reduction.
  • Driver-Centric M Cockpit: M Leather steering wheel with customizable dual red M1/M2 drive setup buttons.

M Drive Setup Sequence

  1. Step 1: Select engine mapping between Efficient, Sport, or Sport Plus.
  2. Step 2: Configure chassis stiffness from smooth road compliance to track-level firmness.
  3. Step 3: Adjust the M Steptronic transmission shift speed across Drivelogic stages 1, 2, or 3.
  4. Step 4: Calibrate the 10-stage M Traction Control system to match driver skill and tarmac conditions.
  5. Step 5: Save your ideal track calibration directly to the red M1 steering wheel toggle.
