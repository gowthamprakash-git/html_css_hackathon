The BMW M2 Coupe is engineered for purists who demand immediate connection with tarmac. Built with a compact wheelbase, aggressively flared wheel arches, and near-perfect 50:50 axle weight balance, the G87 M2 delivers razor-sharp front-end precision and playful rear-axle adjustability.

At its core sits the legendary 3.0-liter BMW M S58 twin-turbocharged inline-six motor — the very same powerplant engineered for its larger M3 and M4 siblings. Tuned to 453 horsepower and 406 lb-ft of torque, this high-revving powerhouse revs eagerly to a thrilling 7,200 RPM redline.

Enthusiasts can choose between a purist 6-speed manual gearbox with automatic rev-matching for total analog control, or the lightning-fast 8-speed M Steptronic transmission with Drivelogic. Power is routed exclusively through an Active M Differential on the rear axle for maximum exit traction.

Inside, lightweight M Carbon bucket seats save 24 pounds of weight, locking driver and passenger securely during high-G lateral cornering. The curved digital display features dedicated M Mode displays, tire temperature telemetry, and a 10-stage drift analyzer.
